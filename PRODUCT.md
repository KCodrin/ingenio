# Ingenio by Tutor Consulting

- **What:** marketing site for Tutor Consulting (Nichelino, TO): sicurezza sul lavoro, formazione, HACCP, privacy/cyber, medicina del lavoro, compliance, organizzazione.
- **Audience:** owners and managers of Italian SMEs; secondary: children/schools (didactic area).
- **Story:** Ingenio (the small hero with a shield = awareness, prevention, the team's expertise) vs Hazard (the unmanaged risk nobody sees coming — not a monster, it simply waits). Both appear in every section of the site.
- **Tone:** clear, confident, warm. Less text, more image and contrast. Italian.
- **Direction (chosen by client, 2026-11-03):** dark storytelling world — deep green-black, Ingenio's green light vs Hazard's amber shadow.
- **Assets pending:** official Hazard illustration (placeholders in place: `.hazard-ph`); children's didactic material.
- **Assumptions:** inferred from the brief and the old site copy; no analytics or user research.
